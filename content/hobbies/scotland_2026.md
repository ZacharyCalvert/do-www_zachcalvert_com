My wife and I decided to make the trek to Oban, Scotland for the scotch, the views, the people, and the cooler weather.  Texas in summertime is miserably hot.  

We traveled by plane, then by train, then by boat to see the sites, meet the people, see the cows, and eat the food.  It was a wonderful trip with amazing people and incredible sites.  We took and Outlander tour visiting all over filming sites for the hit show Outlander.  We managed to see Doune Castle used in filming of the _Monty Python and the Holy Grail_.  I toured the Oban distillery, established in 1794.  It was such an amazing vacation that I took the time to capture it to watch years from now.

Absolutely amazing time.
