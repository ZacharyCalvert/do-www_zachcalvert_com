## Designing, Modeling, and Securing RESTful APIs

I have scheduled the release of my book "_Designing, Modeling, and Securing RESTful APIs_" on Kindle Direct Publishing November 16th 2026.  I [purchased a block of ISBN numbers](https://www.myidentifiers.com/) and have assigned ISBN 979-8-9825062-0-7 to the manuscript.

My book is available for purchase at https://a.co/d/0ihitHBG or just hit me up for a free copy and maybe I'll reply with the DRM-free EPUB.

Publishing a book, really any kind of book, is something I have had on my bucket list since I was twenty.  I'm not under any delusion that it will be a world changing book, or even a best seller, but I hope it can make a difference for a few engineers out there.  I'm proud of the content and think I kept it long enough to cover the topic, but short enough to not drone and enter boring tech book territory.

If you are a fellow engineer and enjoy the book, I'd love to hear from you.  Happy building!
