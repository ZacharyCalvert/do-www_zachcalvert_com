## Relaunching My Site

I've had www.ZachCalvert.com disabled for a few years since joining McKesson and am getting it back up in action.  I'm giving [Digital Ocean](https://www.digitalocean.com/) a whirl for hosting with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) as my content engine.  The code is [here](https://github.com/ZacharyCalvert/do-www_zachcalvert_com) and I'm impressed with everything so far.  It feels great to be able to deliver a themed static content site in less than a day.

My plan for this site is to be a mix of personal and professional content.  From [Hobbies](/hobbies/) to content about the [book](https://github.com/ZacharyCalvert/restbook) I'm working on, I intend for this to be a mix bag of what I'm up to and where to find me.
