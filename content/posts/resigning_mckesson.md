## Departing McKesson

After serving as Principal Solutions Architect leading a team of over 400 engineers managing a 90 billion USD pharmaceutical distribution revenue stream, I transitioned to a DevSecOps Principal position on a team of security professionals guiding 4,000+ application engineers to practicing better application security posture.  While I'm proud of the work we accomplished, our team quickly moved from envision and execute to operate and maintain with limited opportunity for professional growth.  Our team had shrunk from four DevSecOps professionals down to two individual contributors, including myself.

I resigned from McKesson in August 2026 with my last day being September 15th, 2026.  During this transition period, I completed a handoff and delivered training material for my colleagues including the company uses for Kubernetes, GitHub Actions, Docker, and Grafana.  I prepared documentation regarding our in-house built custom GitHub Applications as well as GitHub Action data pipelines feeding content into Azure Databricks and Power BI.  I still text leadership regularly, and they understand why I chose to depart.

Since resigning I am nearly finished writing my book [Designing, Modeling, and Securing RESTful APIs](/DMSR_preview.epub), have managed to improve my sleep, lose over 20 pounds (9 kg), and have started learning some additional technology stacks including [Hugo](https://gohugo.io) and [NextJS](https://nextjs.org/).
